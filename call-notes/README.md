## Quick reference — recurring concepts

Definitions that come up across calls, so you don't have to re-derive them:

- **EBL limit (§461(l))** — Excess Business Loss cap. Max business loss a W-2 earner can use against wage income. ~$256k single / ~$512k married filing jointly. Inflation-indexed, so verify the current-year figure.
- **NOL** — Net Operating Loss. Anything above the EBL cap rolls forward and can offset up to 80% of next year's income, stacked on top of a fresh EBL limit.
- **STR exception (§469)** — Real estate is passive by default. Average rental period of 7 days or less + material participation moves it to non-passive, which is what lets losses hit W-2 income.
- **Material participation** — The hours test that makes the above work. Commonly cited as 100+ hours; the stricter reading is 100+ hours *and* more than any other individual, including the management company.
- **Cost segregation** — Study that quantifies how much of a property's basis can be depreciated on an accelerated schedule.
- **K-1** — The form the investor receives showing their allocated loss. Entered as non-passive.
